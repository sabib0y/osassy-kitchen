High-Conversion Landing Page Layout
1️⃣ Hero (Value Proposition)

Your hero is already visually strong. It just needs slightly sharper messaging.

Structure:

Authentic Nigerian Meals Delivered Weekly

Freshly cooked dishes like jollof rice, ayamase and egusi,
delivered straight to your door.

[ Start Your Meal Plan ]  ← primary CTA
[ Browse Menu ]

Key improvements:

subscription-first wording

clearer promise

two CTAs

Consider also adding 3 micro trust badges under the CTA:

✔ Freshly cooked weekly
✔ Flexible subscription
✔ Authentic Nigerian recipes

Small but psychologically powerful.

2️⃣ How It Works (Critical Missing Section)

People need to quickly understand the subscription.

Simple visual steps:

How Osassy’s Kitchen Works
1️⃣ Choose your meals
Pick from our weekly Nigerian menu.

2️⃣ We cook fresh
Prepared with authentic ingredients.

3️⃣ Delivered to your door
Enjoy restaurant-quality meals at home.

Icons here work well.

This removes friction immediately.

Why This Works Better

Your current page is information-driven.

This structure is conversion-driven.

It follows the psychology:

Attract
↓
Explain
↓
Show food
↓
Build trust
↓
Offer options
↓
Convert
One Honest UX Observation

Your menu section is currently too dense.

It looks like a PDF pasted into a webpage.

That’s fine for a menu page.

But on a landing page it kills momentum.

You want:

big food imagery → curiosity → click to menu

Not:

wall of text menu → cognitive overload
