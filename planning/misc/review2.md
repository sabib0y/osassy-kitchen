What Still Feels Slightly Off

Now we get into the real UX polish stuff.

1️⃣ The Hero Message Hierarchy

Right now the hero reads:

Authentic Nigerian Meals
Delivered Weekly

This is good, but the hierarchy could be stronger.

Currently the main headline is a bit generic.

Better would be something like:

Authentic Nigerian Meals Delivered Weekly

Then the subtext:

Freshly cooked dishes like jollof rice, ayamase and egusi delivered straight to your door.

That way the value proposition is clearer in one line.

2️⃣ The Popular Dishes Section Is Visually Weak

The idea is good.

But the UX problem is:

They look like small thumbnails, not dishes.

Food websites usually go big and appetising.

Example improvement:

Instead of

6 small cards

Do

3 large cards
or
2 rows of 2 larger cards

Make them more like:

Jollof Rice + Chicken
Ayamase + Rice
Egusi + Pounded Yam
Pepper Soup

Big imagery drives appetite.

3️⃣ The “Why Subscribe” Icons Are Too Generic

Right now they look like:

standard UI icons

small visual presence

That section is selling the core product, so it should pop more.

Possible improvement:

Instead of just icons:

Fresh Weekly Meals
Authentic Nigerian Flavours
Flexible Subscription

Make them mini feature cards.

Like:

[icon]

Fresh Weekly Meals
Prepared fresh in small batches
every week.

Give them a little more visual weight.

4️⃣ The Services Cards Are Strong But Slightly Confusing

You currently have:

Weekly meal plans
One-off orders
Event catering

But the first card is already the main product.

That creates a subtle duplication with the hero.

Two options:

Option A (cleaner)

Rename:

Weekly Meal Plans
One-Off Orders
Event Catering

to

Meal Subscriptions
Bulk Orders
Event Catering
Option B (even cleaner)

Remove the first card entirely.

Then show:

Other Services
• One-off orders
• Event catering

This keeps subscription front and centre.

5️⃣ Testimonials Look Slightly Generic

The quotes are good, but visually they look like placeholder cards.

Food businesses benefit from:

faces

avatars

Instagram-style images

Example improvement:

⭐ ⭐ ⭐ ⭐ ⭐
“The jollof tastes like home.”

— Funke A.

Add tiny circular profile pictures.

Small detail but increases trust massively.

6️⃣ The Delivery Section Is Good But Feels Slightly Empty

Currently it’s just text.

You could make this section more useful with:

Delivered Across London

• Fresh weekly meals
• Reliable delivery
• Flexible subscription
• No long-term contracts

Even better:

Add a map graphic or delivery radius.

7️⃣ One Micro UX Issue (small but important)

Your two CTAs repeat everywhere:

Start My Meal Plan
Browse Menu

That’s good — consistency is good.

But one extra CTA would be useful:

Start My Meal Plan
Browse Menu
See Weekly Menu

Because subscription users will want to see what they’ll actually get this week.
